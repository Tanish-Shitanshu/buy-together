# Video script (under 3 minutes)

Record the live site in one browser window. No music. Keep the PayPal approval pop-up on screen for a few seconds only. Speak slowly. Total target: under 3:00 (the table ends at 2:50; speak a little faster in the middle rows).

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Hero price $0.95 for sparkling water, rate card | "Ravi runs a small shop in Rohtak. One can costs him $0.95. At a thousand cans the price drops to $0.70, but he only needs four hundred." |
| 0:15 | Join panel, hold button, PayPal pop-up | "On Buy Together, strangers pool their orders. Ravi joins with four hundred cans. PayPal holds his money. It is held, not spent." |
| 0:35 | Stack fills with five shops, price tick moves | "Four more shops join. Each has its own separate hold. The price drops as the group grows." |
| 0:50 | Group chat: Ravi types in Haryanvi, switch to Meena (Tamil), message appears in Tamil. Tap speaker | "Ravi writes in Haryanvi. Meena in Chennai reads it in Tamil, and can hear it spoken. Everyone uses the app in their own language." |
| 1:10 | Click "Ask Munim". Negotiation rounds appear | "Now Munim, our agent, negotiates for the whole group. It never goes above anyone's walk-away price." |
| 1:30 | FastBulk quote with red highlights, Pause checks, shared warning list | "A second seller offers $0.38 and asks for an advance on a personal UPI. Pause checks every offer first. Price, seller, pressure words, money, group. It blocks this one, and nine other groups already reported this seller." |
| 1:55 | Review panel: each buyer's yes, family helper box, copy link | "Each buyer says yes. Ravi asks his mother to check first. She opens a link and hears the deal in Haryanvi." |
| 2:10 | Undo bar counting down, Pay now, receipt with saved total | "There is an Undo window before any money moves. Then PayPal captures each buyer's exact share, and releases the rest. The group saved six hundred and twenty seven dollars." |
| 2:20 | Click Ronnie, say or type "Ronnie, how much will I pay?" (switch speech on once) | "Ronnie follows me around. I call its name in my own language and it answers in the same language, and can speak it." |
| 2:30 | Delivery day board, drag a stop | "Munim plans the delivery truck. Drag a stop to change the time." |
| 2:38 | Order book and PayPal ledger (AG Grid), Invite on WhatsApp, New deal with Channel3 prices | "Every hold, spend and refund is in one live ledger. Invite shops with a link. Start a deal for anything, with real shop prices from Channel3." |
| 2:50 | Close on hero | "Buy Together. Buy together. Pay safely." |

Checklist before recording: Reset demo, set viewing as Ravi, language Haryanvi, PayPal sandbox buyer logged in on the same browser.
